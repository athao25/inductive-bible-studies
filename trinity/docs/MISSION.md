# Trinity Course · Mission

First doctrine course on the site (previous courses walk books; this one walks a doctrine through its passages).

## Goal

By the end, the student can:

1. State the doctrine in one sentence (White's definition) and in the seven statements.
2. Defend each of the three pillars from at least two passages, from memory.
3. Name the historic errors and say which text each dies on.
4. Pray, worship, and read Scripture along the doctrine's shape (Eph 2:18).

## Method

Same inductive discipline as the book courses: every lesson is one passage, read twice in the
student's ESV before the lesson, then observation → interpretation → application. The only addition
is **correlation**: after passages have spoken in context, unit recaps assemble what they teach
together. Creeds arrive last (lesson 22), as fences around finished exegesis, never as sources.

## Structure

24 lessons, 5 units:

- Foundations (1-2): how to study a doctrine; the big picture.
- Unit 1 · One God (3-6): Deut 6, Isa 44-45, Gen 1, recap.
- Unit 2 · The Son Is God (7-12): John 1, John 5, Phil 2, Col 1, Heb 1, recap.
- Unit 3 · The Spirit Is God (13-17): John 14, John 16, Acts 5, 1 Cor 2, recap.
- Unit 4 · Three Persons, One God (18-21): Matt 3, Matt 28, Eph 1, recap.
- Unit 5 · Guarding and Living (22-24): heresies and creeds, Eph 2:18, capstone.

Eight memory verses (Deut 6:4 → Eph 2:18) build the three-pillar argument as a recitable set.

## Companion book

James R. White, *The Forgotten Trinity* (Bethany House, 1998; rev. 2019), read chapter by chapter
alongside the units (reading assignments in each lesson's "Primary source" card). Quotations in
lessons are brief attributed excerpts for commentary/teaching; the site never reproduces the book.
MacArthur Study Bible notes remain the per-passage primary source, as in the other courses.
