Inductive doctrine course

The Doctrine of the Trinity

24 lessons · ~25 min per lesson · one doctrine, built passage by passage

How this works
This is a doctrine course, but the method has not changed: every lesson still walks one passage inductively. Read the passage twice in your ESV first and write your own observations, then work through observation, interpretation, and application, with recall quizzes along the way. The difference is the destination. Instead of tracing one book from start to finish, the lessons gather the passages from which the church drew one doctrine, so that by the end you can state the Trinity from the texts themselves, not from a diagram. Use the sidebar to jump to any lesson or reference page and to mark lessons complete. Progress is saved in this browser only.
Where the course goes
Five units. One God: the Old Testament's fierce monotheism, which the doctrine never softens. The Son is God: five passages where the New Testament says it outright. The Spirit is God: a person, not a power, and God, not a creature. Three Persons, One God: the passages where all three stand together. Then the boundary lines: the early errors, the creeds that answered them, and what the doctrine means for prayer, worship, and assurance.
