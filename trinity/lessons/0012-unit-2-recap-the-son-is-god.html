Unit 2 Recap: The Son Is God

~25 min · No new passage · Consolidating John 1, John 5, Philippians 2, Colossians 1, Hebrews 1

Five witnesses have testified. Today you retrieve their testimony, assemble it against Unit 1's standard, and say the verdict in your own words. This recap matters more than most: the deity of Christ is where the doctrine of the Trinity is most often attacked, and after today you should be able to defend it from five passages without notes.

Recall: the whole unit

The unit in one frame

Unit 1 defined deity by marks God swears he shares with no one: sole creation, sustaining sovereignty, salvation, judgment, worship, the name, the first and the last. Unit 2 then watched the New Testament, written by monotheist Jews, hand every one of those marks to Jesus of Nazareth, in five independent streams: a Gospel prologue, a controversy narrative, a hymn the churches sang, a letter to a confused congregation, and a sermon built of Old Testament quotations. No single proof-text carries the doctrine; that is its strength. The case is cumulative and everywhere, load-bearing in texts about humility (Phil 2), pastoral sufficiency (Col 1), and Sabbath controversy (John 5), which is exactly what you would expect if the Son's deity were not a late theory but the ground floor of apostolic faith. And note what was not concluded: not that the Son is the Father (John 1:1's "with" forbids it; the Father sends, the Son is sent), and not that there are two gods (the Shema stands; "to the glory of God the Father"). Deity without confusion, distinction without division: two pillars now stand, and the pattern of the third is already visible.
