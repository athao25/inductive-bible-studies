Hebrews 1: The Radiance of His Glory

~25 min · Passage: Hebrews 1 (ESV, read first!)

Observation check

Compare your list. Strong observations on this passage include:

  • The Son is God's final speech (vv. 1-2). Many times, many ways, through the prophets; but in these last days, "by his Son". Not another installment; the last word.
  • Seven statements, one Son (vv. 2-3). Heir of all things; agent of creation ("through whom also he created the world"); radiance of the glory of God; exact imprint of his nature; upholder of the universe by the word of his power; purifier of sins; seated at the right hand of the Majesty.
  • "Radiance", not reflection (v. 3). The Son is to the Father as the shining is to the sun: not a second light, and not a mirror, but the glory itself streaming forth. Same nature, never separate, yet distinguishable.
  • "Exact imprint of his nature" (v. 3). The word behind "imprint" is the die-stamp image: everything God is, the Son expresses precisely.
  • He upholds the universe "by the word of his power" (v. 3). Colossians 1:17 said it of him; here he does it the way God does anything, by speaking.
  • "Sat down" (v. 3). Priests stood daily; this purifier finished and sat, at the right hand of the Majesty on high.
  • The angel comparison (vv. 4-14). Seven Old Testament quotations, all bent to one question (v. 5): "to which of the angels did God ever say...?" Written to people tempted to slot Jesus into the highest creaturely category available, exalted spirit-being. The whole chapter exists to forbid the middle shelf.
  • "Let all God's angels worship him" (v. 6). Angels refuse worship everywhere else in Scripture (Rev 19:10, 22:8-9); here they are commanded to give it.
  • The Father addresses the Son as God (vv. 8-9). "But of the Son he says, 'Your throne, O God, is forever and ever'", quoting Psalm 45, with "God, your God, has anointed you" in the next breath: one called God, anointed by his God. The seed-pattern of Unit 1, now in full flower.
  • Psalm 102, transferred (vv. 10-12). "You, Lord, laid the foundation of the earth in the beginning" — a psalm addressed to YHWH, applied by the Father to the Son: creator, unchanging, "your years will have no end".

Interpretation

Radiance and imprint: the vocabulary Nicaea needed

He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power. Hebrews 1:3, ESV

Verse 3 gives the two images the church leaned on for centuries, and it is worth seeing why they are so exact. Radiance: the shining of a light is not a second light, nor is it the light's creation; it is of the light, with the light, inseparable from it, as old as it. There has never been a sun without its shining, and never a Father without his Son; "light from light", the creed will say, is this verse compressed. Exact imprint: whatever the die is, the stamp expresses, feature for feature; nothing of God's nature is missing from the Son, or he could not be its exact imprint. Notice both images hold deity and distinction in one picture, which by now you should recognize as the New Testament's signature move: the radiance is not the glory-source, the imprint is not the die, the Son is not the Father, and yet what the one is, the other is. John said it with "with God / was God"; Paul with "form of God"; Hebrews with radiance and imprint. Three vocabularies, one doctrine.

The argument from worship, and the closed middle shelf

The chapter's engine is the angel contrast, and its edge is liturgical: "let all God's angels worship him" (v. 6). Scripture's rule is absolute, worship God alone (Ex 20:3-5; and angels themselves enforce it, Rev 22:8-9); so a command for all angels to worship the Son either breaks Scripture's deepest rule or identifies the Son as within the one God worship belongs to. Then verses 8-12 remove all doubt about which, with the Father himself doing the naming: "Your throne, O God, is forever", and "You, Lord, laid the foundation of the earth", Psalm 102's address to YHWH placed on the Son. Assemble Unit 2's case, now complete: the Word who was God and made all things (John 1); the Son who works, raises, judges, and is honored as the Father (John 5); the form of God who receives Isaiah's every knee (Phil 2); the firstborn in whom all fullness dwells (Col 1); the radiance whom angels worship and the Father calls God and Lord (Heb 1). Five independent witnesses, every mark of exclusive deity from lesson 4 accounted for: name, creation, sustaining, salvation, judgment, worship, "the first and the last" (Rev 1:17). Pillar 2, as it concerns the Son, is established. The recap will make you say it in your own words.

Application

  • About God God's last word is not a doctrine but a Son. Every earlier word was preparing for him; no later word will improve on him.
  • About you The one upholding the universe by his word "made purification for sins" for you, personally, then sat down because it was finished. Your standing rests on completed work.
  • Do Memory verse 5 activates today: Hebrews 1:3. Learn it in its three movements: who he is, what he did, where he sat. Review John 1:1 and Philippians 2:10-11 aloud.
