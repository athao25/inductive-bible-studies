Unit 3 Recap: The Spirit Is God

~25 min · No new passage · Consolidating John 14, John 16, Acts 5, 1 Corinthians 2

The forgotten person, remembered. Four passages have established the two claims most Christians have never been asked to prove: the Spirit is a person, and the Spirit is God. Retrieve them today, because pillar 2 now stands complete, and Unit 4 will immediately build on it.

Say it without looking

Before any quiz, take paper and try these three from memory. Then check yourself against the answers below.

  1. Recite John 14:26 (memory verse 6).
  2. List as many marks of the Spirit's personhood as you can recall, with a reference for each.
  3. State the two-verse argument of Acts 5, and the analogy of 1 Corinthians 2:11, each in one sentence.

Recall: the whole unit

The unit in one frame

The unit made two claims in sequence, because the second is meaningless without the first. Person: the upper room gave the Spirit a personal office, personal acts, personal pronouns against the grammar's grain, and the dignity of being "another of the same kind" as the divine Son. A force can be used; only a person can be obeyed, followed, lied to, grieved. God: Peter's equation (to the Spirit = to God), the wilderness test-formula applied to him, the indwelling presence that makes believers God's temple, and Paul's analogy placing him on the inside of the divine life, comprehending the depths of God as only God can. Along the way the unit kept discovering what Unit 2 discovered about the Son: the choreography of distinct persons in one seamless action, the Father sending in the Son's name, the Son sending from the Father, the Spirit taking from the one undivided fullness ("all that the Father has is mine... he will take what is mine"). Pillar 2 is complete: the Father is God, the Son is God, the Spirit is God, and none of them is either of the others. What remains is to see all three in one frame at once, which is exactly what Unit 4's passages provide, and then to say with the church what all of it together must mean.
