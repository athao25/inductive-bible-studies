Memory Verses

Eight anchors, ESV, full text below: the doctrine in portable form. A verse goes "active" at its lesson; lessons keep reviewing earlier ones on a spaced schedule. Together they are the three-pillar argument from memory.

1. Deuteronomy 6:4 · Lesson 3 (Unit 1)

"Hear, O Israel: The LORD our God, the LORD is one." Deuteronomy 6:4, ESV

Why this one: pillar 1's charter. Everything the course concludes must fit inside this sentence, and does.

2. Isaiah 44:6 · Lesson 4 (Unit 1)

Thus says the LORD, the King of Israel and his Redeemer, the LORD of hosts: "I am the first and I am the last; besides me there is no god." Isaiah 44:6, ESV

Why this one: exclusive deity defined by God himself, and the exact clause the risen Jesus speaks in Revelation 1:17.

3. John 1:1 · Lesson 7 (Unit 2)

In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. John 1:1, ESV

Why this one: deity and distinction in three clauses; the verse every denial of the doctrine must first do something with.

4. Philippians 2:10-11 · Lesson 9 (Unit 2)

...so that at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, in heaven and on earth and under the earth, and every tongue confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father. Philippians 2:10-11, ESV

Why this one: Isaiah 45:23, God's least shareable oath, fulfilled at Jesus' feet, to the Father's glory.

5. Hebrews 1:3 · Lesson 11 (Unit 2)

He is the radiance of the glory of God and the exact imprint of his nature, and he upholds the universe by the word of his power. After making purification for sins, he sat down at the right hand of the Majesty on high. Hebrews 1:3, ESV

Why this one: the vocabulary Nicaea needed (light from light), plus the finished work, in one verse.

6. John 14:26 · Lesson 13 (Unit 3)

"But the Helper, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you." John 14:26, ESV

Why this one: the Spirit's personhood, office, and the sending choreography of the three persons in a single sentence.

7. Matthew 28:19 · Lesson 19 (Unit 4)

"Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit." Matthew 28:19, ESV

Why this one: one name, three persons: the doctrine in the grammar of every Christian's baptism.

8. Ephesians 2:18 · Lesson 23 (Unit 5)

For through him we both have access in one Spirit to the Father. Ephesians 2:18, ESV

Why this one: the doctrine as the shape of prayer: through the Son, in the Spirit, to the Father. The course in eleven words.

The ninth "verse": the definition

"Within the one Being that is God, there exists eternally three coequal and coeternal persons, namely, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit." James R. White, The Forgotten Trinity, ch. 2 · assigned in Lesson 2

Not Scripture, and never treated as such; memorized as the course's working summary of what the eight verses jointly teach.

How to memorize (and keep it)

  • First day: read the verse aloud 10 times, then say it from memory 5 times.
  • Spacing: review tomorrow, then at 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. Lessons will prompt these reviews.
  • Retrieve, don't reread: always attempt from memory first; only then check the ESV. Effortful recall is what builds retention.
  • Say the reference before and after the verse so the address sticks with the words.
  • As a set: once all eight are active, practice running them in order as the three-pillar argument: 1-2 (one God), 3-5 (the Son), 6 (the Spirit), 7-8 (three in one name, one access).
