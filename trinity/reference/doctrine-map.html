Doctrine Map

The whole course on one page: the definition, the pillars, the seven statements, the errors, and the texts. Built for review and for teaching someone else.

The definition

"Within the one Being that is God, there exists eternally three coequal and coeternal persons, namely, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit." James R. White, The Forgotten Trinity, ch. 2

Grammar to keep: Being answers what God is (one answer); person answers who God is (three answers). One and three in different senses: mystery, never contradiction.

The seven statements

  1. There is one God.
  2. The Father is God.
  3. The Son is God.
  4. The Holy Spirit is God.
  5. The Father is not the Son.
  6. The Son is not the Spirit.
  7. The Spirit is not the Father.

Hold all seven and you have the doctrine. Drop 1: tritheism. Drop 2-4 (for Son or Spirit): Arianism / pneumatomachianism. Drop 5-7: modalism.

The distinctions (never confuse the persons)

  • The Father sends the Son (John 3:17) and, with the Son, sends the Spirit (John 14:26; 15:26). The Father is of none.
  • The Son is eternally begotten of the Father (John 1:14, 18): an eternal relation, not an event. "Begotten, not made."
  • The Spirit proceeds from the Father (John 15:26), sent by Father and Son.
  • The persons speak to and love one another (Matt 3:17; John 17:24), which no mere mode of one person can do.
  • Order of persons is real; inferiority of nature is not (John 5:23; 14:28 rightly read, lesson 13).

The life of the doctrine

  • Prayer: through the Son, in the Spirit, to the Father (Eph 2:18; Rom 8:26).
  • Baptism: one name, three persons (Matt 28:19).
  • Benediction: grace of the Son, love of the Father, fellowship of the Spirit (2 Cor 13:14).
  • Assurance: chosen by the Father, redeemed by the Son, sealed by the Spirit (Eph 1:3-14).
  • Unity: one access, one household; the church's oneness images the persons' oneness (Eph 2:18-22; John 17:21).
  • Love: God did not begin to love at creation; salvation is adoption into eternal fellowship (John 17:24-26).
