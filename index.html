How this works
Every lesson walks one passage with the inductive method: read the passage twice in your ESV first and write your own observations, then work through observation, interpretation, and application, with recall quizzes along the way. Inside a course, use the sidebar to jump to any lesson or reference page and to mark lessons complete. Progress is saved in this browser only, per course.
