# Matthew Study Resources

## Knowledge

- [ESV Bible online (ESV.org)](https://www.esv.org/Matthew+1/)
  Primary study text with free cross-references and reading plans. Use for: every daily reading.
- [Grace to You: John MacArthur sermon archive on Matthew](https://www.gty.org/library/resources/sermons-library/scripture/40)
  Full free audio/transcripts of MacArthur's verse-by-verse preaching through all of Matthew, starting with [Matthew, Vol. 01](https://www.gty.org/sermons/series/7001/matthew-vol-01). Use for: expositional model per passage.
- [MacArthur Study Bible (owned, in Olive Tree)](https://www.olivetree.com/)
  Verse notes, book introduction, and outline for Matthew. Use for: quick context checks after your own observation step, never before it.
- MacArthur Study Bible Matthew notes, local copy: `reference/macarthur-notes.html`
  The user's personal copy of their owned MSB Matthew notes, formatted with per-chapter anchors and a lens-caveat header. Private use only. Use for: linking a specific chapter's note from a lesson (e.g., `macarthur-notes.html#ch24`).
- [Apologia Church sermons (Jeff Durbin) on Apologia Studios](https://apologiastudios.com/shows/apologia-church/)
  Durbin preached through Matthew at Apologia, including the Sermon on the Mount and a postmil, partial-preterist Matthew 24. Use for: kingdom/optimistic-eschatology handling of Matt 5-7, 13, 24-25. The archive is chronological with no per-book index; pull specific sermons per passage.
- [Alpha and Omega Ministries / The Dividing Line (James White)](https://www.aomin.org/)
  White's exegetical work; [Dividing Line archive](https://www.aomin.org/aoblog/category/the-dividing-line/). Use for: careful text-driven argument at hard texts (e.g., Matt 24:36 textual question, Matt 28:19 and the Trinity).
- [Calvin, Commentary on a Harmony of the Evangelists (CCEL, free)](https://ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom31/calcom31.i.html)
  Classic Reformed exposition of Matthew, Mark, and Luke; 3 volumes (calcom31-33), free full text. Use for: the historic Reformed reading of any passage.
- [R. T. France, _The Gospel of Matthew_ (NICNT, Eerdmans)](https://www.eerdmans.com/9780802825018/the-gospel-of-matthew/)
  The standard conservative one-volume commentary; strongest on structure and flow. Use for: structure and flow questions. Not owned; the recommended purchase.
- [D. A. Carson, _Matthew_ (Expositor's Bible Commentary, rev. ed.)](https://www.amazon.com/Matthew-Mark-Expositors-Bible-Commentary/dp/0310268923)
  Best on theological argument and problem passages. Use for: second opinion beside France. Not owned; optional.
- [Leon Morris, _The Gospel According to Matthew_ (PNTC, Eerdmans)](https://www.eerdmans.com/9780851113388/the-gospel-according-to-matthew/)
  Warm, readable conservative exposition. Use for: accessible alternative if France feels heavy. Not owned; optional.
- [Blue Letter Bible](https://www.blueletterbible.org/esv/mat/1/1/)
  Free Strong's-linked word study tools on the ESV. Use for: English-based Greek word studies (e.g., basileia, dikaiosyne, teleios).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local church (user is a member)
  Primary community. Lessons end with occasional "discuss with your pastor or group" prompts tied to the passage.
- [r/Reformed](https://www.reddit.com/r/Reformed/)
  Well-moderated Reformed subreddit. Use for: questions your local church can't get to, resource recommendations.

## Gaps

- No owned full-length Matthew commentary; France NICNT is the recommended single purchase.
- MacArthur's [4-volume MNTC Matthew set (Moody)](https://www.moodypublishers.com/matthew-1-28-macarthur-new-testament-commentary-four-volume-set) is not owned. The free GTY sermon archive covers the same ground, so buy only if a print commentary is wanted.
