# Mission: Expositional Study of the Gospel of Matthew (All 28 Chapters)

## Why
To know Jesus Christ more deeply and personally through a complete, careful, verse-by-verse pass through Matthew, seeing him as the promised Messiah-King and the fulfillment of the Old Testament, and learning sound inductive Bible study method along the way. The goal is a changed walk with God, not just information: hearing the King's words (the five discourses) and obeying them (Matt 7:24, 28:20).

## Success looks like
- Can read any passage in Matthew and identify its context, structure, and main point without help
- Can practice the inductive method (observation, interpretation, application) unaided on a new passage
- Can explain Matthew's storyline, the five-discourse structure, and key themes (kingdom of heaven, fulfillment of the OT, Son of David, discipleship, the church, the Great Commission) from memory
- Has memorized ~10 key verses, roughly one per unit
- Can trace how Matthew presents Jesus as the fulfillment of the prophets' King, Servant, and Immanuel promises
- Regularly discusses what is being learned with people at church

## Constraints
- 20-30 minutes per day, daily cadence
- ESV is the study translation; owned tools are the MacArthur Study Bible and Olive Tree Bible Software
- New to serious/structured study: lessons must teach method foundations, not assume them, and define Gospel-specific terms (Synoptic, discourse, kingdom of heaven, fulfillment formula) on first use
- Reformed framework; postmillennial (Durbin-style) home position on kingdom passages, with MacArthur (premil/futurist) and White (amil) divergences flagged honestly at the big passages, especially the Olivet Discourse (Matt 24-25)
- Workflow per session: read the passage in ESV first and jot observations, then open the lesson

## Out of scope
- Greek language study (English-tools word study only)
- Academic critical scholarship beyond briefly answering the authorship/date and Synoptic Problem questions
- Formal apologetics training (touch on resurrection and prophecy-fulfillment objections when the text raises them, no more)
- Harmonization projects across all four Gospels (cross-reference parallels as needed, no more)
