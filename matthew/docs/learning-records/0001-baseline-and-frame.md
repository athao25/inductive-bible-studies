# Baseline established: new to structured study, Reformed postmil frame chosen

User is a regular believer but new to formal inductive method; observation/interpretation/application must be taught from zero (lesson 0001 does this before any exposition begins), and terms defined on first use. Matthew is a standalone course with no assumed prior study track. The user deliberately chose a postmillennial home lens (Durbin) while wanting MacArthur (futurist premil) and White (amil) divergences flagged, which means the fork passages (5:17-20, ch. 13, 16:18-19, ch. 24-25, 28:18-20) need comparative treatment, not single-view assertion. The Olivet Discourse is the decisive one: the home reading is partial-preterist and the MacArthur Study Bible notes argue hard the other way, so those lessons must argue from the text rather than from labels.

**Implications**: Lesson difficulty floor is low for method, but theological motivation is high; do not dumb down doctrine, only scaffold method. Gospel-specific vocabulary (Synoptic, discourse, kingdom of heaven, fulfillment formula, typology) is new and must be introduced deliberately. First skill milestone to verify: unaided observation list on a fresh passage, targeted at lesson 0005 (Matt 2:1-12), including noticing the fulfillment formula without prompting.
