Memory Verses

One anchor verse per stretch of the book, ESV, full text below. A verse goes "active" when its unit begins; lessons keep reviewing earlier ones on a spaced schedule.

1. Matthew 1:21 · Unit 1 (ch. 1-4)

"She will bear a son, and you shall call his name Jesus, for he will save his people from their sins." Matthew 1:21, ESV

Why this one: the name Jesus defined; the whole Gospel's mission in one line.

2. Matthew 4:4 · Unit 1 (ch. 1-4)

But he answered, "It is written, 'Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that comes from the mouth of God.'" Matthew 4:4, ESV

Why this one: the King defeats temptation with Scripture; so must we.

3. Matthew 5:17 · Unit 2 (ch. 5-7)

"Do not think that I have come to abolish the Law or the Prophets; I have not come to abolish them but to fulfill them." Matthew 5:17, ESV

Why this one: the hinge of the Sermon and of the whole Bible's storyline; fulfillment, not abolition.

4. Matthew 6:33 · Unit 2 (ch. 5-7)

"But seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things will be added to you." Matthew 6:33, ESV

Why this one: the ordered heart; the antidote to anxiety.

5. Matthew 9:13 · Unit 3 (ch. 8-10)

"Go and learn what this means: 'I desire mercy, and not sacrifice.' For I came not to call the righteous, but sinners." Matthew 9:13, ESV

Why this one: spoken at Matthew's own dinner table; the heart of the King for sinners.

6. Matthew 11:28-30 · Unit 4 (ch. 11-13)

"Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light." Matthew 11:28-30, ESV

Why this one: the great invitation; the only place Jesus describes his own heart.

7. Matthew 16:24-25 · Unit 5 (ch. 14-18)

Then Jesus told his disciples, "If anyone would come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross and follow me. For whoever would save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for my sake will find it." Matthew 16:24-25, ESV

Why this one: discipleship defined the moment the cross enters the story.

8. Matthew 22:37-39 · Unit 6 (ch. 19-23)

And he said to him, "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind. This is the great and first commandment. And a second is like it: You shall love your neighbor as yourself." Matthew 22:37-39, ESV

Why this one: the whole law on two hooks; the King's summary of righteousness.

9. Matthew 24:35 · Unit 7 (ch. 24-25)

"Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away." Matthew 24:35, ESV

Why this one: the durability of the King's words; the Olivet Discourse's still point, echoing Isaiah 40:8.

10. Matthew 28:18-20 · Unit 8 (ch. 26-28)

And Jesus came and said to them, "All authority in heaven and on earth has been given to me. Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, teaching them to observe all that I have commanded you. And behold, I am with you always, to the end of the age." Matthew 28:18-20, ESV

Why this one: the Great Commission; the book's destination and the church's marching orders.

How to memorize (and keep it)

  • First day: read the verse aloud 10 times, then say it from memory 5 times.
  • Spacing: review tomorrow, then at 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. Lessons will prompt these reviews.
  • Retrieve, don't reread: always attempt from memory first; only then check the ESV. Effortful recall is what builds retention.
  • Say the reference before and after the verse so the address sticks with the words.
