Unit 2 Recap: New Birth and Living Water

~25 min · No new passage · Consolidating John 3-4

Two chapters, two long conversations, and one doctrine underneath both. Today you retrieve it and say it in your own words. Retrieval is harder than rereading; the difficulty is what makes it stick.

Say it without looking

  1. Recite John 3:16 (memory verse 2), then say John 1:14 (memory verse 1) after it.
  2. Explain the new birth to an imaginary friend in four sentences, using 3:3, 3:6, 3:8, and 1:13.
  3. Name the two conversation partners of these chapters and one thing each of them misunderstood literally.
Check your answers
(2) Something like: no one enters God's kingdom without being born again; flesh only produces flesh, so no human effort or heritage can cross the line; the Spirit works freely like wind, seen by effects rather than controlled; and children of God are born not of human will but of God. (3) Nicodemus took "born again" as re-entering the womb; the woman took "living water" as a stream or a labor-saving supply. John stages literal misunderstanding to force the spiritual meaning into the open.

Recall: the whole unit

Two people who could not be more different, one diagnosis and one cure. The respectable man cannot produce the birth; the ruined woman cannot dig the well. Both gifts are the same gift under two pictures, because the Spirit who causes the new birth (3:5-8) is the living water Jesus gives (4:14 with 7:39). And notice how the unit ends: with an official who has neither Nicodemus' theology nor the woman's need for rescue from shame, only a dying son and a word to believe. Three social worlds, one requirement.
