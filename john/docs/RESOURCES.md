# John Study Resources

## Knowledge

- [ESV Bible online (ESV.org)](https://www.esv.org/John+1/)
  Primary study text with free cross-references and reading plans. Use for: every daily reading.
- [Grace to You: John MacArthur sermon archive on John](https://www.gty.org/library/resources/sermons-library/scripture/43/John)
  Full free audio/transcripts of MacArthur's verse-by-verse preaching through John. Use for: expositional model per passage.
- [MacArthur Study Bible (owned, in Olive Tree)](https://www.olivetree.com/)
  Verse notes, book introduction, and outline for John. Use for: quick context checks after your own observation step, never before it.
- MacArthur Study Bible John notes, local copy: `reference/macarthur-notes.html`
  NOT YET CREATED. When the user pastes their owned MSB John notes (per-chapter anchors ch01-ch21, lens-caveat header, same format as the Matthew and Isaiah copies), lessons can deep-link them. Private use only; already covered by the repo gitignore.
- [Apologia Church sermons (Jeff Durbin) on Apologia Studios](https://apologiastudios.com/shows/apologia-church/)
  Durbin's preaching archive. Use for: Reformed, kingdom-oriented handling of belief, new birth, and the sovereignty passages. The archive is chronological with no per-book index; pull specific sermons per passage.
- [Alpha and Omega Ministries / The Dividing Line (James White)](https://www.aomin.org/)
  White's exegetical work; [Dividing Line archive](https://www.aomin.org/aoblog/category/the-dividing-line/). Use for: this course's specialist witness on John: John 1:1 against Arian readings (his book The Forgotten Trinity), John 6 on sovereign grace (The Potter's Freedom), and the John 7:53-8:11 manuscript question.
- [Calvin, Commentary on the Gospel According to John (CCEL, free)](https://ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom34/calcom34.i.html)
  Classic Reformed exposition; 2 volumes (calcom34-35), free full text. Use for: the historic Reformed reading of any passage.
- [D. A. Carson, _The Gospel According to John_ (PNTC, Eerdmans)](https://www.eerdmans.com/9780802836830/the-gospel-according-to-john/)
  The standard conservative one-volume commentary on John; strongest on theology and argument. Use for: the first check on any hard passage. Not owned; the recommended purchase.
- [Leon Morris, _The Gospel According to John_ (NICNT, Eerdmans)](https://www.eerdmans.com/9780802825049/the-gospel-according-to-john/)
  Warm, thorough conservative exposition. Use for: second opinion beside Carson. Not owned; optional.
- [Andreas Köstenberger, _John_ (BECNT, Baker)](https://bakerbookhouse.com/products/john-baker-exegetical-commentary-on-the-new-testament-9780801026447)
  Strong on structure, background, and the feasts. Use for: setting and structure questions. Not owned; optional.
- [Blue Letter Bible](https://www.blueletterbible.org/esv/jhn/1/1/)
  Free Strong's-linked word study tools on the ESV. Use for: English-based Greek word studies (e.g., logos, pisteuo, meno, parakletos).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local church (user is a member)
  Primary community. Lessons end with occasional "discuss with your pastor or group" prompts tied to the passage.
- [r/Reformed](https://www.reddit.com/r/Reformed/)
  Well-moderated Reformed subreddit. Use for: questions your local church can't get to, resource recommendations.

## Gaps

- No owned full-length John commentary; Carson PNTC is the recommended single purchase.
- No local copy of the MSB John notes yet; paste them into `reference/macarthur-notes.html` to enable per-chapter deep links from lessons.
- MacArthur's [2-volume MNTC John set (Moody)](https://www.moodypublishers.com/john-macarthur-new-testament-commentary-set/) is not owned. The free GTY sermon archive covers the same ground, so buy only if a print commentary is wanted.
