# Mission: Expositional Study of the Gospel of John (All 21 Chapters)

## Why
To know Jesus Christ more deeply and personally through a complete, careful, verse-by-verse pass through John, seeing him as the eternal Word made flesh, the Son of God who gives eternal life to all who believe (John 20:31), and sharpening the inductive study method learned in the Matthew course. The goal is a changed walk with God, not just information: believing more deeply, abiding more nearly (John 15:4-5), loving more visibly (John 13:34-35).

## Success looks like
- Can read any passage in John and identify its context, structure, and main point without help
- Can practice the inductive method (observation, interpretation, application) unaided on a new passage
- Can explain John's storyline, the signs/glory structure, and key themes (believe, life, light, glory, the hour, witness, the Spirit) from memory
- Can name the seven signs and the seven "I am" sayings with their chapters
- Has memorized ~10 key verses, roughly one per unit
- Can defend the deity of Christ from John's own text (1:1, 8:58, 20:28) in plain conversation
- Regularly discusses what is being learned with people at church

## Constraints
- 20-30 minutes per day, daily cadence
- ESV is the study translation; owned tools are the MacArthur Study Bible and Olive Tree Bible Software
- Lessons stand alone: method foundations are retaught in lesson 0001 (adapted from the Matthew course), and John-specific terms (sign, Logos, "I am" sayings, Paraclete, Book of Signs/Glory) are defined on first use
- Reformed framework; on John's great doctrines (Christ's deity, sovereign grace in John 6 and 10) MacArthur, White, and Durbin agree, and lessons say so plainly. Live checkpoints handled from the text: 3:5 (born of water and Spirit), 6:52-58 (bread of life and the Supper), 7:53-8:11 (the manuscript question), 15:1-8 (fruitless branches), 20:22-23 (the Spirit and forgiveness)
- Workflow per session: read the passage in ESV first and jot observations, then open the lesson

## Out of scope
- Greek language study (English-tools word study only)
- Academic critical scholarship beyond briefly answering authorship/date and the John 7:53-8:11 textual question
- Formal apologetics training (touch on deity-of-Christ objections where the text raises them, no more)
- Harmonization projects across all four Gospels (cross-reference parallels as needed, no more)
