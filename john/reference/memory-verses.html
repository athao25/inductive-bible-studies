Memory Verses

One anchor verse per stretch of the book, ESV, full text below. A verse goes "active" when its unit begins; lessons keep reviewing earlier ones on a spaced schedule.

1. John 1:14 · Unit 1 (ch. 1-2)

And the Word became flesh and dwelt among us, and we have seen his glory, glory as of the only Son from the Father, full of grace and truth. John 1:14, ESV

Why this one: the incarnation in a single sentence; the whole Gospel is this verse unpacked.

2. John 3:16 · Unit 2 (ch. 3-4)

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life." John 3:16, ESV

Why this one: the most famous verse in the Bible, learned here in its context: the bronze serpent, the light, and the verdict.

3. John 6:35 · Unit 3 (ch. 5-6)

Jesus said to them, "I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst." John 6:35, ESV

Why this one: the first "I am" saying; coming and believing defined as eating and drinking.

4. John 8:12 · Unit 4 (ch. 7-8)

Again Jesus spoke to them, saying, "I am the light of the world. Whoever follows me will not walk in darkness, but will have the light of life." John 8:12, ESV

Why this one: spoken under the great lamps of the Feast of Booths; the pillar of fire claims to be standing in the temple court.

5. John 10:27-28 · Unit 5 (ch. 9-10)

"My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me. I give them eternal life, and they will never perish, and no one will snatch them out of my hand." John 10:27-28, ESV

Why this one: the good shepherd's grip; the security of the sheep rests on him, not on them.

6. John 11:25-26 · Unit 6 (ch. 11-12)

Jesus said to her, "I am the resurrection and the life. Whoever believes in me, though he die, yet shall he live, and everyone who lives and believes in me shall never die. Do you believe this?" John 11:25-26, ESV

Why this one: spoken at a graveside before the greatest sign; resurrection is a person, not only an event.

7. John 14:6 · Unit 7 (ch. 13-17)

Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me." John 14:6, ESV

Why this one: the exclusive claim at the heart of the upper room; comfort and confrontation in one line.

8. John 15:5 · Unit 7 (ch. 13-17)

"I am the vine; you are the branches. Whoever abides in me and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing." John 15:5, ESV

Why this one: the Christian life in one picture; fruit comes from abiding, never from straining.

9. John 16:33 · Unit 7 (ch. 13-17)

"I have said these things to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation. But take heart; I have overcome the world." John 16:33, ESV

Why this one: the last sentence Jesus speaks to the disciples before praying; realism and triumph held together.

10. John 20:30-31 · Unit 8 (ch. 18-21)

Now Jesus did many other signs in the presence of the disciples, which are not written in this book; but these are written so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name. John 20:30-31, ESV

Why this one: the book's own purpose statement; you practiced observation on it in lesson 1, and you close the course carrying it.

How to memorize (and keep it)

  • First day: read the verse aloud 10 times, then say it from memory 5 times.
  • Spacing: review tomorrow, then at 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. Lessons will prompt these reviews.
  • Retrieve, don't reread: always attempt from memory first; only then check the ESV. Effortful recall is what builds retention.
  • Say the reference before and after the verse so the address sticks with the words.
