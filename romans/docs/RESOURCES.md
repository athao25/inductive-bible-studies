# Romans Study Resources

## Knowledge

- [ESV Bible online (ESV.org)](https://www.esv.org/Romans+1/)
  Primary study text with free cross-references and reading plans. Use for: every daily reading.
- [Grace to You: John MacArthur sermon archive on Romans](https://www.gty.org/library/resources/sermons-library/scripture/45/Romans)
  Full free audio and transcripts of MacArthur's verse-by-verse preaching through Romans. Use for: an expositional model per passage, and the fullest single free commentary on the letter.
- [MacArthur Study Bible (owned, in Olive Tree)](https://www.olivetree.com/)
  Verse notes, book introduction, and outline for Romans. Use for: quick context checks after your own observation step, never before it.
- MacArthur Study Bible Romans notes, local copy: `reference/macarthur-notes.html`
  NOT YET CREATED. When the user pastes their owned MSB Romans notes (per-chapter anchors ch01-ch16, lens-caveat header, same format as the Matthew and Isaiah copies), lessons can deep-link them. Private use only; already covered by the repo gitignore.
- [Alpha and Omega Ministries / The Dividing Line (James White)](https://www.aomin.org/)
  White's exegetical work; [Dividing Line archive](https://www.aomin.org/aoblog/category/the-dividing-line/). Use for: this course's specialist witness on Romans 9. His book *The Potter's Freedom* is a sustained exposition of 9:6-24 and the surrounding argument.
- [Apologia Church sermons (Jeff Durbin) on Apologia Studios](https://apologiastudios.com/shows/apologia-church/)
  Durbin's preaching archive. Use for: Reformed, kingdom-oriented handling of grace, election, and the ethics of chapters 12-14. The archive is chronological with no per-book index; pull specific sermons per passage.
- [Calvin, Commentary on Romans (CCEL, free)](https://ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom38/calcom38.i.html)
  Calvin called Romans the door to "the whole treasure of Scripture". Free full text. Use for: the historic Reformed reading of any paragraph.
- [Luther, Preface to Romans (free text)](https://www.ccel.org/l/luther/romans/pref_romans.html)
  Short, and the document that warmed Wesley's heart in 1738. Use for: reading lesson 0001's account of "the righteousness of God" in Luther's own words.
- [Douglas Moo, _The Epistle to the Romans_ (NICNT, Eerdmans)](https://www.eerdmans.com/9780802871442/the-epistle-to-the-romans/)
  The standard conservative one-volume commentary; strongest on the argument's flow and on 3:21-26. Use for: the first check on any hard passage. Not owned; the recommended purchase.
- [John Murray, _The Epistle to the Romans_ (NICNT, older edition)](https://www.eerdmans.com/9780802825766/the-epistle-to-the-romans/)
  Careful Reformed exegesis, particularly on 5:12-21 and chapter 9. Not owned; optional second opinion.
- [Thomas Schreiner, _Romans_ (BECNT, Baker)](https://bakerbookhouse.com/products/romans-baker-exegetical-commentary-on-the-new-testament-9780801097447)
  Strong on the Jew-Gentile setting and on the New Perspective questions. Not owned; optional.
- [Leon Morris, _The Apostolic Preaching of the Cross_](https://www.eerdmans.com/)
  The book that settled the propitiation-versus-expiation question against C. H. Dodd. Use for: the background to lesson 0012's checkpoint. Not owned; optional.
- [Blue Letter Bible](https://www.blueletterbible.org/esv/rom/1/1/)
  Free Strong's-linked word study tools on the ESV. Use for: English-based Greek word studies (dikaioo, logizomai, hilasterion, sarx, nomos, kauchaomai).

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local church (user is a member)
  Primary community. Lessons end with occasional "discuss with your pastor or group" prompts tied to the passage, and chapters 12-16 are written to be applied inside a specific congregation.
- [r/Reformed](https://www.reddit.com/r/Reformed/)
  Well-moderated Reformed subreddit. Use for: questions your local church cannot get to, resource recommendations.

## Gaps

- No owned full-length Romans commentary; Moo (NICNT) is the recommended single purchase.
- No local copy of the MSB Romans notes yet; paste them into `reference/macarthur-notes.html` to enable per-chapter deep links from lessons.
- The New Perspective on Paul is deliberately out of scope as a topic; if it becomes a live question, Schreiner's commentary is the entry point that engages it without abandoning the Reformation reading.
- MacArthur's MNTC Romans volumes (Moody) are not owned. The free GTY sermon archive covers the same ground, so buy only if a print commentary is wanted.
