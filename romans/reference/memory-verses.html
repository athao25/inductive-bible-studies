Memory Verses

Twelve anchors across the letter, ESV, full text below. A verse goes "active" in the lesson noted; lessons keep reviewing earlier ones on a spaced schedule. Romans is worth more memory work than any other book: these twelve are the load-bearing walls.

1. Romans 1:16-17 · Unit 1, activates in lesson 0004

For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek. For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith for faith, as it is written, "The righteous shall live by faith." Romans 1:16-17, ESV

Why this one: the theme of the letter. Everything from 1:18 to 11:36 unpacks these two verses, and the Reformation began with a man staring at the phrase "the righteousness of God".

2. Romans 3:23-24 · Unit 1, activates in lesson 0010

for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, and are justified by his grace as a gift, through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus Romans 3:23-24, ESV

Why this one: the diagnosis and the cure in a single sentence, and a warning against quoting verse 23 without verse 24.

3. Romans 3:28 · Unit 2, activates in lesson 0013

For we hold that one is justified by faith apart from works of the law. Romans 3:28, ESV

Why this one: the sentence the Reformation was fought over. Short enough to keep, sharp enough to settle arguments.

4. Romans 5:1 · Unit 2, activates in lesson 0017

Therefore, since we have been justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ. Romans 5:1, ESV

Why this one: the first "therefore" after the doctrine, and the reason justification is not an abstraction. Note the past tense of the verdict and the present tense of the peace.

5. Romans 5:8 · Unit 2, activates in lesson 0018

but God shows his love for us in that while we were still sinners, Christ died for us. Romans 5:8, ESV

Why this one: the proof of God's love is dated and located, not felt. "While we were still sinners" rules out the idea that he waited for us to improve.

6. Romans 6:23 · Unit 3, activates in lesson 0022

For the wages of sin is death, but the free gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord. Romans 6:23, ESV

Why this one: wages versus gift, in one line. Learn it with its context so it stays an argument about slavery and freedom rather than a tract slogan.

7. Romans 8:1 · Unit 3, activates in lesson 0026

There is therefore now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus. Romans 8:1, ESV

Why this one: the answer to the cry of 7:24. Three of its words carry the weight: therefore (because of everything since 3:21), now (already, not at the judgment), and no (not "less").

8. Romans 8:28-30 · Unit 3, activates in lesson 0029

And we know that for those who love God all things work together for good, for those who are called according to his purpose. For those whom he foreknew he also predestined to be conformed to the image of his Son, in order that he might be the firstborn among many brothers. And those whom he predestined he also called, and those whom he called he also justified, and those whom he justified he also glorified. Romans 8:28-30, ESV

Why this one: the most quoted promise in the letter, with the ground underneath it that makes it true. Never learn verse 28 without 29-30; the chain is the reason.

9. Romans 8:38-39 · Unit 3, activates in lesson 0030

For I am sure that neither death nor life, nor angels nor rulers, nor things present nor things to come, nor powers, nor height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God in Christ Jesus our Lord. Romans 8:38-39, ESV

Why this one: the summit of the letter, and the verse to have ready in the dark. Say the whole list; the sweep is the point.

10. Romans 10:9-10 · Unit 4, activates in lesson 0035

because, if you confess with your mouth that Jesus is Lord and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. For with the heart one believes and is justified, and with the mouth one confesses and is saved. Romans 10:9-10, ESV

Why this one: the clearest statement in Scripture of what a person must believe and say, and a text you will use in conversation more than any other in the letter.

11. Romans 11:33-36 · Unit 4, activates in lesson 0039

Oh, the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments and how inscrutable his ways! "For who has known the mind of the Lord, or who has been his counselor?" "Or who has given a gift to him that he might be repaid?" For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be glory forever. Amen. Romans 11:33-36, ESV

Why this one: where eleven chapters of argument land. If your doctrine of election does not end here, you have not finished the chapter.

12. Romans 12:1-2 · Unit 5, activates in lesson 0041

I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect. Romans 12:1-2, ESV

Why this one: the hinge from doctrine to life. The "therefore" is the largest in the Bible; keep the mercies of chapters 1-11 attached to it whenever you quote it.

How to memorize (and keep it)

  • First day: read the verse aloud 10 times, then say it from memory 5 times.
  • Spacing: review tomorrow, then at 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month. Lessons will prompt these reviews.
  • Retrieve, don't reread: always attempt from memory first; only then check the ESV. Effortful recall is what builds retention.
  • Keep the connector. In Romans the first word is often the argument: therefore (5:1; 8:1; 12:1), for (1:16; 6:23), but God (5:8). Say it; never start at the pretty part.
  • Say the reference before and after the verse so the address sticks with the words.
